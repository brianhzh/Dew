## FUTURE ARTIFACTS

### 1

- Plant that grows and changes based on quality purchases
- What if mode: user simulates situation based on financial decision
- User inputs salary and income, as well as current savings
- Snowflake API queries data from financial markets as well as stock environments
- Ensure feasibility

### 2

- Snowflake API draw from data and generate future predictions; based off those predictions, we take the average p50 and have it generate an event for the plant
- A subscription would be a pest
- Real life financial decisions simulated through events to plants
- Ex. a big purchase would be a snowstorm for the plant
- Resilience score: generating a user score based on how a user can handle unexpected events based on their current status based on worse case solution
- Ex. temp job lost, with my savings I can survive for this amount of time and what I need to drop to continue, when. Iffy.
- Cannot be loss only — two tracks. Loss track and Gain track.
- Based on purchase or decision, user can input amount. Snowflake generates a view based on that information for loss and gain thru Monte Carlo.
- Settable / attainable gains from information
- Short, mid, long term goals at a time: only three
- Instances of savings after that can expedite the process of a goal of the user’s choice
- System can recommend portion of saving

### 3

- After purchase is made, everything should be geared toward changing long term thinking, give users an example
- Prevent user options
- See results after decisions
- Independence
- Visuals depict a long term effect
- Big purchases won't be felt instantly, rather over a period of time
- The idea of a plant and caring for a living thing encourages ppl to take care of it, a big purchase would visually hurt the plant
- Plant has to be *(source note cuts off here)*
